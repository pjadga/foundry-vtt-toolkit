### Campaign Overview: Grounded Solo Campaign Framework (12 Sessions)

This single-player campaign is designed specifically for one player character (PC) operating in Night City during the Time of the Red (2045). It focuses on low-level, street-tier survival that gradually escalates into deeply personal stakes as the player transforms from a mercenary paying monthly rent (1,000 eb/month) into the defensive anchor of their local community.

---

### Session-by-Session Narrative Arc

#### Act I: Street-Level Survival & Discovery (Sessions 1–4)
* **Session 1: Rent Is Due** — The PC is scraping together cash for rent. They accept a 500 eb salvage gig from local Fixer [[Rook]] to clear scavengers out of an abandoned warehouse in the Old Combat Zone. Inside, the PC retrieves a damaged pre-War military datachip bearing the serial number of their estranged parent.
* **Session 2: Muscle on the Block** — Cyber-gangers hired by [[Vance "Hard-Line" Miller]] attack [[The Overlook]], cutting power lines and harassing tenants. The PC collaborates with neighborhood Medtech [[Doctor Maya Chen]] to repel the gangers (requiring DV 13 Brawling or Handgun checks) and secure basic supplies.
* **Session 3: Night Market Infiltration** — Seeking a rogue Netrunner to decrypt the datachip, the PC visits a local Night Market. While negotiating, they discover Miller is buying heavy industrial demolition equipment and military combat cyberware to forcibly raze [[The Overlook]].
* **Session 4: First Blood & Twist One** — The PC ambushes a demolition crew sent to plant explosives on the building's structural columns. Upon decrypting the datachip, the PC uncovers **Twist 1**: the chip holds an encrypted memory log revealing [[Vance "Hard-Line" Miller]] was their relative's former partner who betrayed and left them to die during the Fourth Corporate War.

#### Act II: Escalation & Internal Betrayal (Sessions 5–8)
* **Session 5: Securing the Turf** — Utilizing Headquarters (HQ) Advancement Rules, the PC spends earned Improvement Points (IP) to fortify [[The Overlook]] with surveillance feeds, reinforced doors (SP 12), and an emergency medical bay.
* **Session 6: Striking the Supply Line** — The PC executes a tactical raid against Miller’s cyberware distribution depot in Heywood to disrupt his mercenary reinforcements, engaging heavily armored guards (SP 11) in close-quarters combat.
* **Session 7: Shadow in the Clinic & Twist Two** — When an enemy strike team bypasses security to target the building's power grid, the PC investigates the security breach and reveals **Twist 2**: [[Doctor Maya Chen]] has been leaking internal building layouts to Miller in exchange for rare immunosuppressants needed for local children.
* **Session 8: The Subterranean Probe** — Confronting Chen leads to a crucial clue: Miller isn't after the real estate above ground, but a sealed subterranean access hatch in [[The Overlook]]'s sub-basement. Breach attempts reveal a locked pre-War Petrochem NET architecture.

#### Act III: The Climax & Legend of The Street (Sessions 9–12)
* **Session 9: Beneath the Red Dirt & Twist Three** — Jacking into the localized sub-basement NET architecture, the PC discovers **Twist 3**: an unmapped pre-DataKRASH AI fragment is currently stabilizing the entire district's jury-rigged power grid. Extracting or removing the vault's core will cause [[The Overlook]]'s foundation to implode.
* **Session 10: Rallied Streets** — With Miller’s heavy strike team deadline 24 hours away, the PC leverages their personal Lifepath contacts, street rats, and converted gang allies to prepare a neighborhood defense network.
* **Session 11: The Siege of The Overlook** — Miller launches a full-scale assault using armored combat vehicles and heavy mercenaries. The PC conducts a multi-stage tactical defense through choked stairwells and barricaded hallways.
* **Session 12: Confrontation & Legacy** — The PC corners [[Vance "Hard-Line" Miller]] inside the subterranean Petrochem vault. After defeating Miller in single combat, the PC re-routes the AI’s power matrix directly to the building's tenants, establishing [[The Overlook]] as a legally protected, self-sustaining community shelter and cementing the PC's legacy as a Street Legend.
